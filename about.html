ABOUT QAMARS

Built to support businesses as they evolve.

QAMARS CONSULTING is a business consulting and financial support firm working with startups, entrepreneurs, SMEs and established businesses across India.

One Partner for All Your Business Consultancy and Compliance Needs

At QAMARS, we offer a comprehensive range of consultancy services tailored to meet the evolving needs of businesses across India. Our expertise spans strategic advisory, tax compliance, financial management, and business setup solutions. Whether you are a startup, SME, or an established enterprise, we are committed to empowering your success with precise guidance, reliable support, and a results-driven approach.

We believe that business support should extend beyond registrations and periodic filings. A growing organisation needs clean financial information, dependable processes, compliance discipline and access to practical advice when important decisions have to be made.

More Than Compliance. A Business Support Partner.

QAMARS CONSULTING brings business consultancy, finance, accounting, compliance and process-oriented support into one integrated service model. The objective is to reduce fragmentation and give business owners a clearer view of what needs to be done, why it matters and how to move forward.

Our Approach

Understand the business before advising the business.

Every organisation has a different operating model, financial position, risk profile and growth objective. We therefore focus on understanding the context before recommending a solution.

We look at where the business is today, where the owner wants it to go, what is limiting progress, which financial or compliance risks exist and which processes need stronger structure.

Our Business Philosophy

Clarity → Compliance → Control → Growth

Clarity: understand the numbers, structure and priorities.

Compliance: establish and maintain a dependable regulatory foundation.

Control: strengthen processes, accountability and financial discipline.

Growth: create a business environment where better information can support better decisions.

What Makes QAMARS Different

We think beyond the assignment. An accounting engagement should lead to better financial visibility. A registration should lead to a stronger setup. A compliance exercise should create order rather than more confusion. A process review should leave the organisation better organised than before.

Our broader model connects Business + Finance + Compliance + Processes + Management.
